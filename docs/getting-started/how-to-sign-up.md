# How to sign up

1、Registration address: https://hk.archiwood.cn/account/sign_up. Open your browser and enter this address to access the registration page.

2、 Enter the registration information as prompted!![image-20261022095214207](../public/images/image-20261022095214207.png)

3、After all information is filled in correctly, click the blue button to complete the registration.

4、After registering, give your account information to the customer service staff.
