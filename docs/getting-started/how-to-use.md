# How to find an article

The sidebar is grouped into Getting Started, Design, Production, and FAQ. Select a section name to see what that group covers, then open an article under it.

If you already know the feature name, or you have an error message, use the search box at the top of the page. Search only includes articles that have been published.

A guide usually answers three things: what to prepare, what to do in order, and what you should see when you are done. Steps that are easy to get wrong are called out on their own.
